# Full Disk Access

macOS protects some folders in your home folder, such as parts of Library, Mail and Safari data. Without permission DiskLens cannot read their sizes, so it skips them and shows a notice in the sidebar saying how many folders it could not read. Your totals are lower than the real usage until you grant access.

DiskLens only reads sizes and names. It never reads file contents.

## Grant access

1. In the first-run guide, click **Open System Settings**. You can also open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Full Disk Access.
2. Switch DiskLens on. If DiskLens is not in the list, drag its icon from the guide into the list.
3. DiskLens notices the change by itself and moves on.

macOS may ask you to quit and reopen an app after you change this setting.

## Continue without it

Choose **Continue without** in the guide. DiskLens works, and the sidebar notice stays until access is granted. DiskLens never asks for an administrator password for scanning or cleanup.

## After an update

macOS ties the permission to the app build. If DiskLens says access is missing after an update, switch it off and on again in the same pane.
