# Licensing

## Free and Pro

Free covers scanning, Explore, every recommendation and every cleanup. Pro adds new capabilities. Safety checks run the same with and without a license, and the free app never stops working.

## Offline license keys

A license is a signed key. DiskLens checks the signature on your Mac against a public key built into the app. The app never contacts a license server, and there is no account or sign-in.

## Activate

1. Open DiskLens, then **DiskLens › Settings…** (<kbd>⌘,</kbd>).
2. Paste your key into the license field.
3. Click **Activate**.

If you have the app installed, the **Open in DiskLens** button on the success page, in your email and on the [activate page](/activate) opens Settings with the key filled in for you to confirm.

## Trial

A trial is a 7-day, fully usable Pro key. Request it on the [trial page](/trial). The key is emailed to you, once per address. A repeat request sends the same key, so the trial does not restart.

## Lost your key

Use the [recover page](/recover). We email every key for that address.

## Major upgrades

A license names the highest major version it unlocks. A Pro 1.x key covers all 1.x updates. A new major version is a paid upgrade, and your key keeps working on the version you bought.

## Refunds

A refund marks the order as refunded. Because keys are checked offline, a key cannot be revoked remotely. See the [terms](/terms).
